04 · Boost or Burn

You asked for a boost or burn video?

I actually made one a few months ago for a Nigerian brand called Akisa (it means rags). They used the popular “rags to riches” phrase to show how customers can use the brand to make money from their preloved pieces.

But simultaneously, the name and messaging could suggest that the brand was selling used “rags” to its buying customers, a particularly risky association in a highly sentimental Nigerian market.

05 · About

I understand the message, the brand and the feed.

I’m Kenny Awe, a Lagos-based social media and brand designer. My work sits between strategy and execution: understanding what a brand needs to say, how it needs to feel, and how that translates into content people will actually stop for.

At Bami Brands, my work spans social design, visual direction and team leadership, with recent expansion into content strategy and writing. Through The Awesome Design Studio, I also direct brand identity work.
